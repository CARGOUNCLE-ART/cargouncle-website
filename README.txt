# Cargo Uncle Website

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js

This is a ready-to-upload static website.

IMPORTANT:
1. Replace "Add your number" and "Add your email" in index.html with your real contact details.
2. The enquiry form is currently a front-end demo. To receive submissions, connect it to your email service, WhatsApp, Formspree, or a backend later.
3. Upload all three files into the public web folder of your hosting.

Domain:
cargouncle.com
