FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Cargo Uncle logistics and relocation services.

What logistics services does Cargo Uncle provide?

We provide PAN India logistics and transport services including FTL, PTL, Packers & Movers, house shifting, office shifting, household transportation, intercity shifting, bike transport and car transport.

Does Cargo Uncle provide PAN India transportation?

Yes. We coordinate transportation and relocation solutions across India based on the route and shipment requirement.

What is FTL and PTL?

FTL (Full Truckload) uses dedicated vehicle capacity for a complete shipment. PTL (Part Truckload) is suitable when a shipment shares vehicle capacity with compatible consignments.

Can I transport a bike or car between cities?

Yes. We offer intercity bike and car transportation with pickup and delivery coordination.

How do I get a quotation?

Fill in the enquiry form or contact Cargo Uncle directly through WhatsApp or phone for a quotation.
