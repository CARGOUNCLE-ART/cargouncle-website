Packers and Movers in India: A Practical House Shifting Guide

Good relocation planning starts before the truck arrives. Use this checklist to prepare household goods for a smoother move.

Make an inventory

List furniture, appliances, cartons and fragile items. Separate items that need special packing and keep important documents and personal essentials with you.

Choose the right packing approach

Use suitable cartons, protective wrapping and secure packing for fragile goods. Clearly identify delicate items and communicate handling requirements before loading.

Confirm pickup and destination access

Check building access, parking, lift availability, loading areas and delivery timing. This information helps the relocation team plan the movement.

Discuss loading and transport

Ask how goods will be loaded, secured and transported. For intercity shifting, confirm the pickup date, destination and expected movement plan.

Keep essentials accessible

Keep identification, medicines, chargers, valuables, important documents and a few days of essentials separate from the main household shipment.

Get a clear quotation

Share the approximate quantity of goods, pickup and delivery locations, floor details and any packing or loading requirements. Ask what services are included in the quotation.
