House Shifting Checklist: Packing, Loading and Delivery

A simple plan can make a household move easier to organise and reduce last-minute issues.

Before packing

Sort items, make an inventory, separate valuables and documents, and decide what actually needs to move.

During packing and loading

Use suitable packing material, label boxes by room and keep fragile items clearly identified. Confirm the loading sequence before the vehicle leaves.

At delivery

Check the inventory, inspect fragile items and confirm that all agreed goods have been delivered before closing the move.
