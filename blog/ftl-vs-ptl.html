FTL vs PTL Transport: Which One Is Right for Your Shipment?

FTL uses a dedicated truck for one shipment, while PTL shares truck capacity with other compatible consignments.

Choose FTL when

Choose Full Truckload when your shipment is large enough to use most of a truck, requires dedicated capacity, or benefits from a more direct movement.

Choose PTL when

Part Truckload is useful for smaller consignments where sharing capacity can reduce transportation cost.

What should you compare?

Compare cargo volume, weight, pickup and delivery requirements, transit expectations and total quoted cost before booking.
