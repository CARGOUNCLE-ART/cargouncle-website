# Cargo Uncle — Positioning Package

## Positioning Statement

For mid-market and enterprise companies in Delhi NCR who need employee, office, or asset relocations handled without disruption, Cargo Uncle is the corporate relocation partner that treats every move like a business operation, not a favor — GPS-tracked transit, insured goods, and a single point of contact from quote to delivery.

Unlike generic local packers-and-movers outfits (including Kashi World and similar players) who compete mainly on price and treat corporate and household moves the same way, Cargo Uncle is built around the accountability, documentation, and SLAs that a business client actually needs — transparent pricing, no hidden charges, and a team that answers to a company, not just a customer.

## Three Message Pillars

### 1. Built for business, not just boxes
Dedicated account handling, GST-compliant billing, bulk/office relocation experience, and SLAs — the difference between a mover who shows up and a vendor a company can actually rely on.

### 2. Nothing hidden, nothing lost
Transparent, itemized pricing with no surprise charges, plus goods insurance and GPS-tracked vehicles so a client always knows where their shipment is and what happens if something goes wrong.

### 3. Delhi NCR expertise, done right the first time
Deep local knowledge of the region's logistics (traffic, access restrictions, high-rise moves) paired with trained crews and proper packing materials — fewer damage claims, fewer delays, fewer excuses.

## Homepage One-Liner

**Primary:**
"Corporate relocation in Delhi NCR, handled like business — not a favor."

**Alternate (warmer tone):**
"Your office move, tracked, insured, and on time — every time."

## Open Question
Buyer segment specified as mid-market/enterprise, but most competitor positioning (Kashi World, etc.) targets household movers. If Cargo Uncle also serves household/individual customers, a separate one-liner and pillar set is recommended for that audience.
